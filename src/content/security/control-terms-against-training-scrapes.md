**In plain terms:** [/terms.txt](/terms.txt) says automated
              bulk collection for model training is not allowed without written consent, while
              ordinary reading, linking, hiring review, and robots-respecting search/citation stay
              welcome.
            

**Why it matters here:** a clear permission statement gives a hook for
              takedown or dispute letters. It does not stop a rude bot by itself.
            

**How:** public terms file plus the robots split above. Weekly access-log
              review for AI user-agents remains an ops habit, not a page feature.
