## How to use this document

1. **Download** and map risk classes (standard / normal / emergency) to your real CAB or chat approvals.
2. **Require** evidence that matches the Change evidence ask sheet.
3. **Define** who may approve emergency changes and how they are recorded after the fact.
4. **Legal / Eng leadership** review before adoption.
5. **Sample** changes quarterly with the ask sheet — policy without samples is theater.

**Not:** a full SDLC or release-engineering handbook.

Download DOCX or PDF for offline editing and review.
