## How to use this document

1. **Choose a format** for the single register of accepted policy deviations.
2. **Every exception needs** owner, compensating control, expiry, and approver.
3. **Review on cadence** (monthly or quarterly). Expired rows close or renew deliberately.
4. **Never** leave “permanent” without an explicit residual-risk acceptance.
5. **Export** before audits as evidence that exceptions are governed.

**Good for:** MFA skip lists, unprotected admin paths, temporary vendor waivers.  
**Not:** a substitute for fixing the control.
