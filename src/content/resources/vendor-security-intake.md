## How to use this document

1. **Choose a format** before a new SaaS or vendor touches production or regulated data.
2. **Requester fills** business use case, data classes, and hosting region.
3. **Security fills** residual risk, required evidence, and go / no-go.
4. **Attach** SOC 2 / ISO / pen-test summaries the vendor already has — do not invent controls.
5. **Store** with the contract packet. Re-review on renewal or material scope change.

**Good for:** first-pass TPRM before Procurement signs.  
**Not:** a full questionnaire, legal terms, or DPA drafting.

Use the web form on the page for a fast working copy, or take the DOCX/PDF version offline.
