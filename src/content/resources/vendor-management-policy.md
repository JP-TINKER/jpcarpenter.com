## How to use this document

1. **Download** and set which vendors are in scope (data class, production touch, criticality).
2. **Require** the Vendor security intake before go-live for in-scope SaaS.
3. **Define** incident notice expectations and who owns re-assessment.
4. **Procurement + Legal** review. Security does not invent contract clauses alone.
5. **Re-assess** on renewal or material scope change.

**Pair with:** Vendor security intake.  
**Not:** a full TPRM program manual or scoring model (extended questionnaire is planned).

Download DOCX or PDF for offline editing and review.
