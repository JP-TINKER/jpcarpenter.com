## How to use this document

1. **Download** and fill severity definitions that match how you actually page people.
2. **Name** roles (commander, comms, legal, exec) — not generic “Security team.”
3. **Wire** detect → report → triage → contain → communicate → recover to your tools.
4. **Legal review** before customer or regulator language ships.
5. **Exercise** once a year; update the policy when the exercise shows drift.

**Pair with:** Incident intake form for the first capture.  
**Not:** a playbook for every product, or breach counsel.

Download DOCX or PDF for offline editing and review.
