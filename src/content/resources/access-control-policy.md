## How to use this document

1. **Download** the policy draft. Replace `[brackets]` with your org, systems, and roles.
2. **Align** joiner / mover / leaver steps to your real tickets and IdP — not aspirational flowcharts.
3. **Set** access-review cadence and who signs.
4. **Send to Legal / HR** before publish. This is a starting structure, not counsel.
5. **Publish** version, owner, and next review date. Pair with the Access and MFA checklist for evidence.

**Ancestry:** structure informed by public FOSS policy packs (e.g. StrongDM Comply, Apache-2.0); wording rewritten.  
**Not:** certified controls or lawyer-approved text out of the box.

Download DOCX or PDF for offline editing and review.
