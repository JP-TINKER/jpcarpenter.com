## How to use this document

1. **Choose a working copy** on the page, or download DOCX/PDF for offline use.
2. **Fill the brackets** — owner, IdP, review window, privileged population.
3. **Send the “Ask for” section** as a dated request to IAM / IdP admins. Require export IDs and timestamps.
4. **Score the pack** with the pass/fail cues before you sign an access-review or MFA claim.
5. **File** the completed sheet + evidence links in your audit binder under Access / MFA.

**Good for:** access reviews, auditor prep, challenging “everyone has MFA” without proof.  
**Not:** legal advice, an audit opinion, or a guarantee that controls work.

Use the web form on the page for quick filling, or take the DOCX/PDF version offline.
