## How to use this pack

1. **Fill** `[Company]`, `[Report channel]`, `[Security owner]`, and `[Escalation path]` before you send anything to staff.
2. **Run** the public lesson: [Authenticated phishing still wins](/tutorials/authenticated-phishing).
3. **Use** the web form on this page (or DOCX/PDF) for the facilitator brief, talk track, roster, and post-checklist.
4. **File** the completed roster and checklist with your awareness records.

**Good for:** a 5–10 minute huddle or async read + short live debrief.  
**Not:** a live phishing simulation platform, LMS completion API, or legal advice.
