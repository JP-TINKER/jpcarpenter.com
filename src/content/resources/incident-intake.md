## How to use this document

1. **Open or download** at first report — before narrative drift.
2. **Capture facts only:** what, when (UTC), systems, who knows, severity sketch.
3. **Assign** incident commander and comms owner on the form.
4. **Do not** use this sheet for blame or HR findings.
5. **Hand off** to the IR runbook; keep this as the opening record.

**Good for:** first 30 minutes of an incident.  
**Not:** post-incident review, legal hold, or customer notification copy.

Use the web form on the page for first capture, or take the DOCX/PDF version offline.
