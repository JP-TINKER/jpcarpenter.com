## How to use this document

1. **Choose a format** when a production change needs audit-ready evidence.
2. **One change per sheet** — ticket ID, risk class, environment.
3. **Ask engineering** for the minimum pack: plan, approval, implement proof, verify, backout.
4. **Refuse “merged to main” alone** as deploy evidence. Prefer pipeline run IDs and timestamps.
5. **File** under Change for the audit period.

**Good for:** spot-checks, PCI change samples, SOC 2 change management walks.  
**Not:** a CAB charter or a full SDLC policy.
