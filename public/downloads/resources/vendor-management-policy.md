## 1. Purpose

Set security rules for IT vendors and partners who can affect the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of `[Organization]` technology or sensitive information.

## 2. Scope

IT vendors and partners in scope of the information security program, and employees/contractors who oversee them.

## 3. Policy

1. Vendors do **not** receive access to information assets until a contract with security controls is signed by the right parties.  
2. Vendors must follow security requirements derived from `[Organization]`’s information security policy (and this document).  
3. Vendor security incidents are documented under the [incident response policy](/resources/incident-response-policy) and forwarded immediately to `[ISM]`.  
4. `[Organization]` meets SLA terms it signs; when terms change, controls are updated accordingly.  
5. Before contract and access, vendors undergo a **risk assessment** covering ICT risk and relevant supply-chain / subprocessor risk.  
6. Vendors must protect, safeguard, and securely dispose of organizational records per legal, regulatory, and contractual rules (including PII where applicable).  
7. `[Organization]` may audit vendors for security and contractual compliance as allowed by contract.

## 4. Minimum pre-contract pack

- Completed vendor security intake  
- SOC 2 / ISO (or equivalent) under NDA when risk is Medium/High  
- DPA / BAA as required  
- Named internal business owner and technical owner  
- Access inventory if the vendor touches our environment
