## Rules of thumb (not AICPA minimums)

- If MFA, access reviews, and change samples are not producing dated artifacts yet, **wait** on Type 2.
- Type 2 needs operating effectiveness **over a period**. Put real `period_start` / `period_end` on every evidence-calendar row.
- Do not invent a “six months required” rule from marketing blogs. Ask your auditor.

## Limits

This worksheet does not start an examination. It does not make you “audit-ready in two weeks.”
