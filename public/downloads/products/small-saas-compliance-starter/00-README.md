# Small SaaS Compliance Starter

**Free pack for 10–50 person cloud SaaS.**  
Routing + fillable tabs. Not a SOC 2 report. Not an ISO certificate.

## Honest limits (read first)

- Does **not** attest SOC 2. A licensed CPA firm issues the report.
- Does **not** certify ISO/IEC 27001 or ISO/IEC 42001.
- Every row must be **true for your systems** and reviewed by management.
- Not legal advice. Not an audit opinion.

## Who this is for

Founders and first security/compliance hires who need to know **which program to run this quarter** and have somewhere to track evidence — without another 80-page policy library.

## What’s in the pack

| File | Job |

## How to use (30 minutes)

1. Read `01-routing.md`. Circle **one** examination for this quarter.
2. Import the CSVs into Google Sheets (File → Import) or Notion (CSV → database).
3. Freeze the header row. Color Status with conditional formatting.
4. Replace every placeholder with facts you can defend.
5. Keep this README’s limits box on your landing copy and in the Sheet.

## Notion tip

Property types that work: Title, Status, Select (framework), Person (owner), Date, Relation (control→evidence), Files, Unique ID. Steal the *shape*, not a marketplace zip.
