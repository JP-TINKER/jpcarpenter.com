# JP mark — round 2 plan

## One sentence

A lettermark for JP Carpenter: clear initials for a reviewer who checks signed claims against running systems — not a shield, tool pun, or stamp cliché.

## Four directions (Airey: words before pixels)

1. **Stem** — One heavy bar; J hook under; P bowl on the side. Favicon-first symbol.
2. **Unit** — Horizontal JP, one stroke weight, free-standing (NASA worm discipline). Logotype.
3. **Pair** — Separate J and P, optically locked, no shared stem. Logotype.
4. **Lockup** — Unit + “Carpenter” as one plate identity.
