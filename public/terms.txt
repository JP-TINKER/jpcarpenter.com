# Terms — jpcarpenter.com

Last updated: 2026-08-31

## Human visitors

You may read, link to, and quote this site with ordinary attribution for personal,
editorial, hiring, or research use.

## Automated access

1. Obey https://jpcarpenter.com/robots.txt. Training and bulk-collection bots listed
   there as Disallow must not fetch site content.
2. Automated scraping, bulk downloading, or reproduction of site content for the purpose
   of training, fine-tuning, or evaluating machine-learning models is not permitted
   without prior written consent from JP Carpenter (jp@jpcarpenter.com).
3. Search indexing and live citation fetches by operators that honor robots.txt and
   that are listed as Allow remain welcome.
4. A machine-readable card with the same facts as the About page is published at
   https://jpcarpenter.com/about.md for polite automated readers. Prefer that file
   over inventing biography.

These terms are a clear statement of permission. They are not a substitute for
technical controls. Some clients ignore robots.txt; hosting-edge gates and log review
